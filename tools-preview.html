FIELD OPERATIONS

Workforce tools

Quick, practical calculators for planning table work and balancing plant counts.

On-device calculations
01

TIME & LABOR

Table time planner

Set the table count, start time and target finish time. The planner deducts breaks and room travel, then calculates the same minutes-per-side target for every employee.

A table aisle has 2 sides. The planner deducts a 20-minute break plus 10 minutes of room travel, and a 40-minute break plus another 10 minutes of room travel (80 minutes total). The resulting minutes-per-side target is identical for every employee.

02

WORKLOAD BALANCE

Plants per person

Divide the plants on one table by the number of employees in the room to estimate each person's workload for that timed section.

The calculation uses plants per table ÷ employees in the room. If it does not divide evenly, the remainder is assigned one whole plant at a time.

03

EVEN DISTRIBUTION

Plants per section

Split a table's plant count into a chosen number of sections. Every section gets a whole number of plants, with the remainder distributed one plant at a time.

Example: 50 plants across 4 sections becomes 2 sections with 13 plants and 2 sections with 12 plants. No section is assigned a fraction of a plant.
